Where the dates come from

Every event's dates come from arknights.wiki.gg, which tracks two dates per event:

  • CN date - when the event ran (or is running) on the CN server.
  • Global date - the same, for the Global/EN server.

An event is marked confirmed as soon as the wiki has a Global date for it, whether that came from an official Yostar announcement or from something datamined out of the client ahead of one. Either source counts the same here.

How estimates are calculated

An unconfirmed event's Global date is just its own CN date plus a lag, but the lag used isn't a flat dataset-wide average - Yostar's localization pace has sped up and slowed down before, and a lag averaged over years of history would react to that far too slowly. Instead:

  1. Every event with both a CN and a Global date gives one CN→Global lag observation, in days.
  2. A lag of zero days (or negative) is dropped - that's the wiki recording the same, or an inverted, date for both servers, not a same-day release.
  3. The lag applied to every current estimate is the median of just the last 10 of those observations, ordered by when each event actually went live on Global - not by calendar time. Recalculated fresh on every update.
  4. Each unconfirmed event's Global date = its own CN date + that lag, independently of any other event.

A trailing window of the 10 most recent releases smooths out any single unusually fast or slow event without averaging in outdated history, so it tracks a real change in pace much faster than an all-time average would - and unlike a fixed calendar window (the last year, say), it doesn't go quiet or noisy just because events happened to ship faster or slower than usual recently.

The CN→Global lag

  • Current lag - the median CN→Global lag across the last 10 confirmed events. This is what every estimate on the page is using right now.
  • Historical lag - the median lag across every confirmed event ever, for comparison. Not used to build any estimate - just a reference point for how far the current pace has drifted from the historical one.

How accurate is this?

Every update, this approach is checked against its own history: for each past confirmed event, the 10-event model is rebuilt using only the events that were confirmed earlier than it, and the resulting estimate is compared to what Global actually shipped.

CN exclusive

An event is marked cnExclusive once both of these are true:

  • It has never had a confirmed Global date.
  • Its estimated window closed more than 30 days ago.

This usually means the event isn't coming to Global at all - the 30-day buffer just keeps a slightly delayed event from getting flagged red too early.

How often this updates

This page rebuilds once a day: a scheduled job re-fetches the wiki data, recalculates every date using the rules above, and republishes the result. Data on this page can be up to a day old.
