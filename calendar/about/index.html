Where this data comes from

Everything on this page comes from arknights.wiki.gg. For every event, it tracks when it ran on CN and when it ran (or is running) on Global. The second the wiki has a Global date for something, it counts as confirmed here — doesn't matter if that's from an actual Gryphline announcement or from someone datamining it out of the client early. Either way, the wiki has it, so this page just uses it instead of guessing.

How the guesses work

For anything without a confirmed Global date yet, we have to guess. The lazy way would be "take the CN date and add the average lag" — but the real lag jumps around a lot event to event, so that tends to leave a weird gap (or overlap) right where a real date meets a guessed one.

So instead, events get walked in CN-date order, and each unconfirmed one just borrows whatever lag the closest earlier event actually had — confirmed, or itself a guess made the same way — and applies that instead of some dataset-wide average. Basically every guess rides on the last real data point rather than a flat number. And the moment an event actually gets confirmed, everything guessed after it snaps back in line with Gryphline's real schedule.

The CN→Global lag

That "Current CN→Global lag" number near the top of the calendar is just the gap between the most recent confirmed event's CN date and its Global date. That's the exact number every current guess is riding on, since the chaining trick above just carries it forward unchanged until the next confirmed date comes along and resets it.

On the rare chance there's no confirmed pair at all to work from yet, the very first guess falls back to the median lag across every event where we do have both dates. You'll see that labeled "Historical CN→Global lag" instead, just so it's clear it isn't the real current number.

CN exclusive

If an event's estimated window closed more than 30 days ago and it still hasn't gotten a real Global date, it gets marked CN exclusive instead of sitting there labeled "upcoming" forever. Usually that just means it's not coming to Global at all, though sometimes it's simply running late — the 30-day buffer exists so a slightly-delayed event doesn't get flagged red too soon.

How fresh this is

This page refreshes itself once a day — a scheduled job pulls the latest from the wiki, redoes all the confirmed/estimated/CN-exclusive math above, and republishes it. So whatever you're looking at is only as fresh as the last run: if something changed on the wiki an hour ago, it might not show up here until tomorrow.
