Where the dates come from

Every event's dates come from arknights.wiki.gg, which tracks two things per event: when it ran (or is set to run) on the CN server, and the same for the Global/EN server. A date counts as confirmed the moment the wiki has a Global-server date for that event — it makes no difference whether that came from an official Gryphline announcement or from something wiki editors datamined out of the client ahead of one. Either way, once the wiki has it, this page treats it as settled and stops estimating it.

Estimated dates

For an event with no confirmed Global date yet, the estimate isn't just "this event's own CN date plus a fixed number of days." The real CN→Global lag varies noticeably from event to event, so applying one flat number everywhere tends to open up an artificial gap (or overlap) right where a confirmed date meets the next estimated one.

Instead, every event is walked in CN-date order, and each unconfirmed event's Global date is set to the nearest earlier event's Global date, plus the CN-side gap between the two. In other words, an estimate inherits whatever lag was most recently actually observed — from a confirmed date, or from an earlier estimate chained the same way — rather than a dataset-wide average. Every newly confirmed date resets the chain, snapping everything estimated after it back onto Gryphline's real schedule from that point on.

The CN→Global lag

The number in the calendar's legend ("Current CN→Global lag used for estimates: ~X days") is the gap between the most recent confirmed event's CN date and its Global date — exactly the lag being applied to every estimate right now, since the chaining above just carries that same gap forward unchanged until the next confirmed date resets it.

Before any confirmed CN/Global pair exists yet to chain from at all, the very first estimate instead falls back to the historical median lag across every event where both dates are already known — shown as "Historical CN→Global lag" instead, on the rare occasion that's the only number available.

CN exclusive

An event that's only ever had an estimated Global date, and whose estimated window closed more than 30 days ago with still no confirmation, is marked CN exclusive instead of sitting there as "upcoming" forever. It usually means the event either isn't coming to Global at all, or is running unusually late — the 30-day grace period exists so an event that's simply a bit delayed doesn't get flagged red too early.

How often this updates

This page rebuilds itself once a day: a scheduled job re-queries the wiki, recomputes every confirmed/estimated/CN-exclusive date using the logic above, and republishes the underlying data. Between runs, what you're looking at is only as fresh as the last run — if the wiki changed very recently, it may take until the next run to show up here.
