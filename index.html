Arknights Projects

A small hub for Arknights tools running on this domain.

Sync your Arknights account

Connect your EN Arknights account once from here to automatically import your account data.

  • Shop Operator History
    Every operator that's rotated through the yellow certificate shop and when. Hover a portrait for the exact date, or click a bar to see the days between appearances. Also has predictions for upcoming shop debuts.
  • EN Event Calendar
    Past and upcoming EN server events with the new operators and outfits each one brings. Confirmed dates once known, estimated dates (and how they've moved) for events not yet confirmed.
  • Operator Planner
    Add one or more operators, set where each one is now and where you want them, and see the total LMD, EXP, and materials needed for their level, Elite promotion, skill level, mastery, and modules, minus what's already in your depot.
  • Operator Details
    Browse any operator's EN/Global release date, stats at every Elite stage, talents, potential upgrades, skills at every level and mastery, modules, and outfits.
